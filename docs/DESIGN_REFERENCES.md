# Midnight Royale: design and motion references

The November 7 visual redesign responds to the team's request for saturated colors and engaging animation. It retains the validated analytics and uses one consistent palette: midnight navy surfaces, cyan observed data, violet modeled comparisons, gold decisions and branding, and coral inspection signals.

Research references, reviewed November 7, 2026:

- [Linear Insights](https://linear.app/insights) and [Linear's UI redesign](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui): disciplined navigation, readable hierarchy, modular metrics, and an overview-to-drilldown path.
- [Vercel Observability](https://vercel.com/products/observability): headline measures connected to charts and underlying detail.
- [Stripe Workbench](https://stripe.com/blog/workbench-a-new-way-to-debug-monitor-and-grow-your-stripe-integration): inspection in context and clear next actions.
- [Grafana Play](https://grafana.com/blog/grafana-play-updates-a-redesigned-homepage-to-celebrate-our-community/): consistent analytical panels and meaningful color semantics.
- [Shipmap](https://www.shipmap.org/): a dark spatial canvas, bright data traces, and user-controlled historical playback. [Information Is Beautiful Awards](https://www.informationisbeautifulawards.com/showcase?page=1&pcategory=winner&type=awards) lists it among its Gold winners.
- [Awwwards data-visualization gallery](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/data-visualization/): interaction and visual-storytelling references.

These are influences, not copied layouts or a claim that one design is universally best. All graphics in the dashboard are original code-native SVG/CSS; no third-party screenshot or brand asset is used.

Motion is bounded and controllable. Charts reveal their actual observations; navigation and hover states use short opacity/transform transitions. The floor replay advances through real monthly aggregates only when the viewer presses Play, labels the selected month, pauses on interaction or a hidden tab, and stops in June. It is explicitly historical, not live telemetry. The motion preference persists locally and honors operating-system reduced-motion settings. The decorative spade and orbital illustration encode no data.
