# A guided path through the decision

The desktop overview retains its particle ace, floating totals, IBM Plex type and graphite/brass palette. The new presentation features connect that opening to the existing analysis; they introduce no new observations or intervention-effect estimates.

## Earnings waterfall

The overview's earnings decomposition now shows signed dollar components as a waterfall. Read the comparison, then use the playback or manual step controls. The final total is the fixed January–June gap for the manager's ten lowest earners. The positive machine component offsets part of the deficit. Published component amounts differ from the published total by one cent because of source rounding; the dashboard discloses this instead of adjusting the data. Attribution is retrospective and descriptive, not a causal estimate.

## Ace to floor

Select **Explore the floor** from the overview. The decorative ace transitions into a dated snapshot with one mark per machine active on June 30, 2025. The snapshot contains 308 machines, including 47 in Back Forty. Eight of the fixed January–June bottom ten are still active; M-1118 and M-1120 are absent on this date. The candidate group, Back Forty and M-1158 inspection signal use separate highlights and explanations. Skip the animation at any time. **Open six-month explorer** resets the explorer to the full January–June period and all zones/banks.

## Guided presentation

Use **Present** in the top bar. Advance manually through the manager's question, earnings decomposition, location evidence, a live scope demonstration, frozen model validation and the priced plan. The scope demonstration has its own controls and does not change the underlying dashboard's filters. Exit at any time or open the relevant detailed view. The guide is interactive; it is not a recorded video submission.

## Reading and access

Analytical labels, chart axes, scope summaries and evidence text are larger. Analysis cards remain flat, while the overview's floating-card appearance is preserved. Keyboard controls, Escape, focus restoration, reduced motion and small-screen layouts are supported. Animation is decorative; the observations and tables remain available without motion.

The current hosted audience is preserved. A recorded walkthrough and Devpost submission are separate deliverables and are not claimed here.
