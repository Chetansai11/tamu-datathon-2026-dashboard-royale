# Dashboard Royale

- **Goal:** Interactive dashboard for The Gilded Ace (fictional 300-machine casino): are the 10 lowest-earning slot machines truly underperforming, or unlucky, badly placed or often broken? Luck, location or a leak?
- **Scored by:** Analytical validity 30%, dashboard and communication 25%, modeling and validation 20%, recommendations and tradeoffs 15%, originality 10%. Judges: change the period, drill into a bank or guest group, find the validation, trace a recommendation to evidence.
- **Submit:** Devpost: public interactive dashboard, video of 2 minutes or less, write-up of 400 words or less, all code, up to 3 priced actions from action_catalog, AI-use disclosure, Claims & Evidence appendix (table/query, result, alternative explanation, limitation).
- **Rules and limits:** Answer the 6 guide questions at minimum. Fictional casino: no claims about real ones, no accusations. Don't design ideas that push heavy losers to gamble more. AI allowed with disclosure. Public outside data allowed if cited. Gaming day is 6 AM to 6 AM. Ratio of sums, not mean of ratios. Time-based holdout only. $100,000 budget for July 1-30 2025, max 3 action types.
- **Definition of done:** Public dashboard link works in a private window, video, write-up, appendix, and code all submitted.
- **Deadline:** Sun Nov 8. Hacking ends 12:00 PM, submit by 11:30 AM. Feature freeze 8:00 AM.
- **Prizes:** 1st Keychron V1 keyboard, 2nd Logitech G703 mouse, 3rd Owalas
